ALL WORKS

Kasatmata

After relocating stores and expanding their services, Kasatmata found their existing website no longer matched who they’d become. The old design lacked visual appeal and offered limited functionality, and it couldn’t showcase the creative work the brand relied on to sell. The owner put it simply: the site wasn’t strong enough to represent them online anymore.

I rebuilt the website around a clearer structure and a stronger visual identity, adding a dedicated album page for seasonal product photography. The result is a site that finally reflects the brand’s creativity, while giving visitors a smoother experience browsing it.
