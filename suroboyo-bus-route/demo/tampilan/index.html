Map display

Drag a row left or right to change its value. Arrow keys nudge by one step, Shift ×5. Double-click a row to reset it. Nothing is saved.

Time of day

Transition speed

Soft radial from the bottom centre. Switch the time of day above to feel it, or compare all speeds side by side in lab/transisi.

Edit routes

Turn it on, pick a line, then drag a corner anywhere. Drag the bar in the middle of a segment to pull out a new bend (Shift: move the whole segment). Double-click a corner to delete it. Turn on the 45° lock to keep it neat like a transit map. Stops do not move.

Hold Alt while dragging to turn snapping off temporarily.

Move stops

Turn it on, then drag a stop on the map. A stop only moves along its own line. Stops shared by two lines move together. Left/right arrows on a focused stop move it 10 m (Shift 50 m).
